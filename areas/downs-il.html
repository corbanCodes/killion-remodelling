How it works

From Your Call to the Job Done

Call, text or send the form

Tell Rick what you have got. Photos help more than descriptions — text them straight to his phone. He answers his own calls, so you are not going through an office.

He comes and looks, free

For anything beyond a small repair he will come out, walk it with you and measure up. There is no charge for the estimate and no sales pitch at the end of it.

You get a straight number

Small jobs get a price on the spot. Bigger ones get it in writing with the materials itemised separately, so you can see what you are paying for.

He does the work himself

Rick is the one who shows up. No crew you have never met, no subcontractor you were not told about. Floors covered, mess cleaned up, and he does not leave a job half finished to go start another one.
