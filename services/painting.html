Painting, Done Clean

The paint is the easy part. The prep, the cut lines and leaving your house tidy are what you are actually paying for.

Handyman & Repairs Painting Decks & Porches Windows & Doors Tile & Carpet Trim & Carpentry Room Additions

Anyone can roll a wall. What separates a paint job that still looks right in five years from one that peels by next summer is everything that happens before the lid comes off — filling, sanding, caulking, priming the patches, and masking like you actually care about the carpet.

Interior work

Furniture moved to the middle and covered, floors run with drop cloths, and the room put back the same evening wherever possible. Holes filled and sanded flush, patches spot-primed so they do not flash through the topcoat, and trim and ceiling lines cut in by hand with a brush rather than taped and hoped for. One coat where one coat genuinely covers, two where it does not — and he will tell you which before he quotes, not after.

Exterior work

Siding washed down and left to dry, loose and flaking paint scraped back, bare wood primed, and gaps and end grain caulked so water has nowhere to sit. Then the colour. Decks, fences, soffit, fascia, shutters and front doors all fall under this too — a freshly painted front door and trim is the cheapest curb-appeal money anyone spends.

Questions

Painting — The Usual Questions

Do you supply the paint or do I?

Either works. Most people have Rick pick it up so the right primer and the right sheen arrive with it, and the paint goes on the bill at cost. If you have already bought yours, or you are set on a particular brand, that is no problem at all.

How long does a room take?

A straightforward bedroom with walls and ceiling is usually a day. Add trim, doors and a second coat and it stretches into a second day. Whole interiors get a schedule up front so you know which rooms are out of action on which days.

Can you match a colour I already have?

Yes. Easiest is a chip off a hidden corner — inside a closet or behind a switch plate — which the paint counter can scan and match. If the original can is still in the basement, even better.

Is exterior painting worth it this time of year?

It depends on the month and the forecast. Central Illinois gives a solid exterior season and Rick will tell you honestly if it is too cold, too wet or too late in the year for the paint to cure properly. A job that will fail by spring is not worth taking your money for.
