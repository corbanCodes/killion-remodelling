Windows & Doors

A window or door is only as good as the fit. Set it out of square and no amount of weatherstripping saves it.

Handyman & Repairs Painting Decks & Porches Windows & Doors Tile & Carpet Trim & Carpentry Room Additions

Draughty windows and doors that have to be shouldered shut are two of the most common complaints in older central Illinois housing stock, and they are two of the most satisfying things to fix. The difference in a room is immediate — you can feel it standing next to the glass in January.

Replacement windows

Measured, ordered, fitted level and square, insulated and sealed around the frame, and trimmed out inside and out so the finish looks original rather than patched. One window because the seal has blown and it fogs up, or a run across the whole front of the house — both are the same job, just a different number of them.

Doors and storm doors

Exterior doors hung so they close with a push rather than a shove, thresholds and sweeps that actually meet the door, deadbolts that line up with their strike, and rotted jamb and brickmould cut out and replaced instead of filled and painted over. Inside, interior doors, bifolds and pocket doors hung and cased. Storm doors and screens fitted or re-screened.

Questions

Windows & Doors — The Usual Questions

Do you order the windows or should I?

Rick will measure and order them, which matters more than it sounds — a replacement window measured off the old frame by eye is how you end up with a gap full of foam. If you have already bought windows, he will fit what you have.

My door closes but the draught is terrible. Replace it?

Often not. A door that still shuts and latches can usually be fixed with new weatherstripping, a proper sweep, a threshold adjustment and the hinges and strike reset. Far cheaper than a new door, and it is worth trying first.

Can one fogged window be done on its own?

Yes. A failed seal in one unit does not mean the rest need doing — it means that one unit failed. Replacing the glass alone is sometimes possible and is cheaper than the whole window.

Will the trim match the rest of the house?

That is the aim and it is the part most people notice. He will match the existing profile as closely as what is available allows, and paint or stain it to blend in rather than leaving you a bright white frame in a room full of oak.
