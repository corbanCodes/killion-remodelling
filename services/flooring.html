Tile & Carpet

A floor is only as flat as what is underneath it. That is the part you never see and the part that decides whether the job lasts.

Handyman & Repairs Painting Decks & Porches Windows & Doors Tile & Carpet Trim & Carpentry Room Additions

Tile cracks and grout lines open up for one reason more than any other: movement underneath. Carpet ripples and seams show for much the same reason. So the first thing Rick does on a floor is pull a corner up and look at what he is tiling or laying over, before anybody talks about colours.

Tile

Floors, backsplashes, tub and shower surrounds, entryways and mud rooms. Subfloor checked and shored up or levelled first, proper backer board or membrane where it belongs, layout set out dry so the cuts land somewhere sensible rather than leaving a sliver at the doorway, then grouted, sealed and silicone where it needs to flex.

Carpet and vinyl plank

Old flooring and tack strip pulled and hauled away, subfloor cleaned, squeaks screwed down while it is open, then new pad and carpet stretched in properly with seams put where the light does not catch them. Luxury vinyl plank is the one most people ask about now and it earns the attention — it handles kitchens, basements, pets and kids better than almost anything at its price.

Questions

Tile & Carpet — The Usual Questions

Can you tile over what is already there?

Sometimes, and sometimes it is the wrong call. It depends what the existing surface is, how well it is stuck down and how much height you can afford to lose at the doors. Rick will look and tell you which, rather than tiling over a problem and handing it back to you in two years.

Who moves the furniture?

He will, as part of the job. Just tell him what is fragile and what you would rather handle yourself, and clear the small stuff off surfaces before he arrives.

Tile or vinyl plank for a kitchen?

Both are good. Tile is harder and cooler underfoot and lasts longest. Vinyl plank is warmer, quieter, much quicker to fit and a lot more forgiving if something gets dropped. He will give you his honest opinion for your room rather than the one with the bigger invoice.

Do you haul the old carpet away?

Yes. Tear-out and disposal are part of the quote, so you are not left with a rolled carpet in the garage until next bulk-pickup day.
