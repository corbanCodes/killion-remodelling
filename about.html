About Rick

Owner-operated means the person who quotes your job is the person who turns up to do it. That is most of what there is to say.

Killion Remodelling is Rick. One van, one phone number, and the same person from the first call to the last bit of clean-up.

He does handyman work and painting, mostly, plus the trades that sit either side of them — decks and porches, windows and doors, tile and carpet, trim and finish carpentry, and room additions when a house needs to get bigger.

Back in Bloomington

Here is the part most businesses would rather leave off a website. Rick was busy in this area a few years ago. Then he was away for a stretch, and he is back now and starting the customer list again from close to nothing.

So no, you probably have not heard of him, and there is no decade of reviews to scroll through yet. Both of those are true and there is no point pretending otherwise. What is also true is that somebody rebuilding a reputation in a town the size of Bloomington–Normal has every reason in the world to do the job properly — word of mouth is the only advertising that has ever worked in this trade, and he knows it.

How he works

Free estimates on anything beyond a quick repair. A straight number rather than a range. Floors covered and the mess cleaned up, because somebody has to live there afterwards. And an honest answer when the cheaper fix is the right one, or when a job genuinely needs a licensed plumber or electrician instead.

He would rather turn down a job he should not take than learn on your house.

What You Get, Plainly

The owner is the one doing the work

You deal with Rick from the first phone call to the last bit of clean-up. Nobody hands you off, nobody turns up who you have not met, and nobody tells you they will have to check with the office.

One person for a list of jobs

Most houses do not need a specialist. They need somebody competent who can paint the bedroom, fix the sink, re-hang the door and sort the deck boards without you coordinating four separate trades.

He will tell you when not to spend money

If the cheaper repair is the right answer, that is what you will hear. If a job needs a licensed plumber or electrician, he will say so instead of taking a run at it.

Free estimates and straight answers

No charge to come and look at anything substantial, no pressure at the end, and a real number rather than a range that quietly doubles once work starts.

Questions

The Things People Ask First

What exactly do you do?

Handyman work and painting, mostly, plus the trades that sit around them — decks and porches, windows and doors, tile and carpet, trim and finish carpentry, and room additions when somebody needs more house. If it is a repair, a refresh or a small build, it is probably on the list.

What do you not do?

Full kitchen and bathroom remodels. Rick will do the tile, the flooring, the trim, the painting and the doors in a kitchen or bathroom, but the whole gut-and-rebuild belongs with somebody who does nothing else. He would rather tell you that than learn on your house.

How far do you travel?

About 50 miles around Bloomington, Illinois — that covers Bloomington and Normal out to Peoria, Champaign, Decatur, Pontiac and Lincoln, and all the smaller towns in between. If you are just outside it, call anyway and ask.

Are you new? I have not heard of you.

Not new to the work, but new again to Bloomington. Rick was busy here a few years back, was away for a stretch, and is building the customer list back up. That is the honest reason you have not seen a truck on your street yet — and the reason he is as keen as he is to do a good job for the first people who call.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Anything beyond a quick small repair gets a free visit, a look and a number. Small repairs get a price over the phone so nobody wastes a trip.

How quickly can you start?

Small jobs are often the same week. Bigger ones get a real start date rather than a vague promise. Call and ask what the next couple of weeks look like — the answer changes.

Can I just text you?

Yes, and most people do. Text (309) 555-0100 with a photo and a sentence about what you need and you will get a straight answer back.
